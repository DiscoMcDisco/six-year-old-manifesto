The first locked door

A child may be blocked before they can even ask.

A pass does not mean handing an unrestricted chatbot to a six-year-old. It means providing a safe and dependable route through a trusted adult, school, library or public service. That route is not available everywhere today.

What we still cannot measure

The gaps are part of the answer.

Hiding uncertainty would make the result look neater and make it less honest. These gaps prevent a pass and show us what needs measuring next.

How the Test stays honest

Research finds the evidence. Fixed rules produce the result.

The questions, weights and scoring rules are fixed. A later run may update the evidence, but it cannot quietly rewrite the Test. Missing figures remain missing, and every published result needs human approval.
